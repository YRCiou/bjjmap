<p><strong>Effective date: 3 September 2026</strong></p>

<p>This Privacy Policy explains how <strong>BJJ Map</strong> ("we", "us") handles information when
you use the website at <a href="https://bjjmap.pages.dev">bjjmap.pages.dev</a> (the "Service").
BJJ Map is an educational Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu knowledge graph and training tool operated by YRCiou
as an individual. The Service is currently in <strong>beta</strong> and under active
development.</p>

<h2>In short</h2>
<ul>
<li>BJJ Map is a <strong>static website</strong>. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no
database.</li>
<li><strong>We collect nothing.</strong> We run no analytics, set no tracking cookies, and operate
no server that could receive your data.</li>
<li>Everything you do is stored <strong>in your own browser's local storage</strong> and never
leaves your device.</li>
<li>We have nothing to sell, share, or hand over, because we never receive it in the first
place.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Who we are</h2>
<p>The Service is operated by <strong>YRCiou</strong>, an individual, at the domain
<strong>bjjmap.pages.dev</strong>. For any privacy question, open an issue at
<a href="https://github.com/YRCiou/bjjmap/issues">github.com/YRCiou/bjjmap/issues</a>.</p>

<h2>Data stored on your device</h2>
<p>The Service stores data in your browser's <strong>local storage</strong> so the training and
game features work. This includes your flashcard and spaced-repetition state, your settings, your
daily progress and streak, your roll history, your saved lists, your challenge and badge progress,
and which states you have explored. It is kept under the key
<code>bjj-neural-progress</code>, among others.</p>
<p><strong>This data never leaves your device.</strong> There is no endpoint it could be sent to.
Clearing your browser's storage deletes it permanently; it is not backed up anywhere, and it does
not follow you to another browser or device.</p>

<h2>Analytics</h2>
<p><strong>There are none.</strong> The Service loads no analytics script, sets no analytics
cookie, and records no page views, events, or identifiers of any kind.</p>

<h2>Cookies</h2>
<p>The Service sets <strong>no cookies of its own</strong>. Third parties may set their own cookies
when their content loads — see below.</p>

that serve it or the content embedded in it:</p>

<h2>Legal bases (GDPR)</h2>
<p>Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies: we do not process personal data, so no
legal basis is engaged on our side. The providers listed above process request metadata under their
own legitimate interest in delivering and securing the content you requested.</p>

<h2>Data retention</h2>
<p>We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Data in your browser's local storage persists
until you clear it.</p>

<h2>Your rights</h2>
<p>Under the GDPR and comparable laws you have rights of access, rectification, erasure,
portability, restriction, and objection. In practice there is nothing for us to act on: we hold no
personal data about you. You can exercise the practical equivalent yourself at any time by clearing
your browser's storage for this site, which erases everything the Service has ever stored.</p>

<h2>Children</h2>
<p>The Service is not directed to children under 16. Since we collect no personal data, we cannot
knowingly or unknowingly collect any from them.</p>

<h2>Changes to this policy</h2>
<p>We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the
<strong>effective date</strong> at the top of this page.</p>
